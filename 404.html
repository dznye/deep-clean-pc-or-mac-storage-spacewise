Questions people ask

Why is my C drive full for no reason?

Usually caches (browser, graphics, package managers), old emulator and virtual-machine images, leftover installers and crash dumps. Much of it is hidden. SpaceWise maps it and labels what's safe to clean.

What can I safely delete when my C drive is full?

Temp files, crash dumps, shader and browser caches and package-manager caches are generally safe because apps rebuild them. Never delete anything in Windows, Program Files or ProgramData. SpaceWise protects those and sends everything to the Recycle Bin first.

How do I find hidden files taking up space?

Scan, then open the Hidden files tab. It lists the biggest hidden folders and files. Hidden doesn't mean junk, so check each item before removing it.

Why is System Data so large on my Mac?

Mostly caches, logs, Xcode data, local snapshots and old device backups. SpaceWise shows the cache, log, Xcode and backup parts. The Mac version is experimental.

Is it safe?

It runs only on your computer and uploads nothing. It only moves items to the Recycle Bin or Trash, only touches paths it showed you, and blocks system and personal folders.
