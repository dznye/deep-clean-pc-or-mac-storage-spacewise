AI to-do list generator: turn a messy brain dump into a plan

By the Fuidzy team · Updated 2026-10-09

A to-do list generator is best at sorting. Dump everything in, let it group, prioritise and estimate, then drag the survivors onto today's timeline. A list with no hours in it is a wish list.

The problem with lists

Most to-do lists are a list that grows. They have no ending, so they quietly become a record of what you haven't done. An AI generator helps by sorting the pile, but the real fix is attaching time to the items that matter and deleting the rest.

Use it for these three jobs

  1. Group: let it cluster related tasks (calls, errands, admin) so you can batch them.
  2. Prioritise: ask it to mark what's urgent, what's important, and what can be dropped.
  3. Estimate: have it guess durations, then correct the guesses you know are wrong.

A prompt to try

Here is my brain dump: [paste]. Group the tasks, mark each as do-today, this-week or someday, estimate minutes for each, and list anything that looks like two tasks hiding in one.

Before and after

Brain dumpAfter the AI sorts it
"sort out taxes, call mum, groceries, finish deck, book flights"Do today: finish deck (90 min). This week: book flights (20 min), groceries (45 min), call mum (15 min). Someday, but break down: taxes (hidden big task).

The most useful line is the last one: AI is good at noticing that "sort out taxes" is really five tasks wearing one name.

Cut the list on purpose

Ask what you can delete, delegate or defer. A shorter list with real slots beats a long one you feel guilty about. Anything that survives should get a time on a real day, because an unscheduled task has a habit of staying unscheduled.

Common mistakes

  • Treating every item as equally important.
  • Keeping tasks that have survived three weeks untouched. Delete them or schedule them.
  • Writing vague tasks like "deal with the car" instead of the next physical action.
  • Using the AI to add more tasks instead of fewer.

Turning vague tasks into next actions

Vague taskNext physical action
Sort out taxesFind last year's return and list the documents I need
Deal with the carCall the garage and ask for the earliest MOT slot
Get fitPut a 30 minute walk in tomorrow at 7:30
Plan the tripDecide dates, then open one booking site for flights

Ask the AI to rewrite every task as a verb plus an object you can start in two minutes. Vague tasks are the ones that stay on a list for a month.

A quick sorting method

  • Do today: important and due, or quick and valuable.
  • Schedule: important but not urgent. Give it a time this week.
  • Delegate or ask for help: someone else could do it.
  • Delete: not important, not urgent.

A five-minute weekly review

  1. Empty your head into the list one more time.
  2. Delete anything you've been avoiding for over three weeks that you now know you won't do.
  3. Put times on this week's real priorities.
  4. Check what repeated and set it to recur instead of re-adding it.
  5. Note one thing that went well. It makes the habit stick.

Prompts for different kinds of lists

A work list

From this list, pick the three tasks that would make today a success. Estimate minutes for each and say what you'd defer if the day gets cut short: [paste].

A household list

Group these into errands (one trip), calls, and online tasks. Order the errands to minimise travel, and mark anything that can be done in a ten-minute gap: [paste].

A project list

Break this project into phases with concrete next actions. Mark anything blocked by something else, and tell me the very first action I can do in under 15 minutes: [paste].

What a good generated list looks like

  • Every item starts with a verb and names a concrete thing.
  • Each has an estimate, even a rough one.
  • The 'today' list is short enough to finish.
  • Blocked items are visibly marked, not mixed in with doable ones.

If the output doesn't look like that, say so in your next message: 'rewrite every item as a verb plus object and add minutes'. Specific feedback improves the second draft far more than starting over.

Common questions

What is an AI to-do list generator?

A tool that turns unstructured notes or a brain dump into a prioritised, estimated task list.

Are AI to-do list makers free?

Many have free tiers. Fuidzy is free and includes an AI model.

How do I stop my to-do list growing forever?

Give each kept task a time slot on a day, and delete or defer anything that doesn't earn one.

How many tasks should be on my daily list?

Most people do better with three to five meaningful tasks scheduled, rather than a long list.

Can AI break a big task into steps?

Yes, that's one of its strongest uses. Ask it to split a vague task into concrete next actions with time estimates.
